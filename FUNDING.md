## Other ways to help

- Report bugs from real boards through the [issue forms](https://github.com/powerdragonfire/platformio.mcp/issues/new/choose). These are the most useful thing you can send.
- Ask and answer questions in [Discussions](https://github.com/powerdragonfire/platformio.mcp/discussions).
- Open a pull request. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) first.
- Star the repository and share it with people who work with PlatformIO.

Thank you for your support.
